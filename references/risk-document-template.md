# Risk Document Template

A companion document to the business plan: the full risk register, in more depth than the plan's own condensed Risks & Mitigations section holds. Produce this whenever there are more risks or more mitigation/monitoring detail worth tracking than fits comfortably in the main plan.

## Summary

The top 3–5 risks in one line each, ranked by how much they threaten the idea's viability — the same ones that appear in the business plan's Risks & Mitigations section, so a reader can jump straight to the ones that matter most.

## Risk Register

One entry per risk, grouped under these categories (omit a category with nothing in it):

- **Market** — demand that doesn't materialize, market shrinking or saturating, timing risk.
- **Competitive** — an incumbent or new entrant closing the gap, price wars, retaliation.
- **Execution** — team, technical, or operational capability to deliver.
- **Financial** — running out of runway, unit economics not working, funding not materializing.
- **Regulatory / Legal** — licensing, compliance, data privacy, industry-specific regulation.
- **Patent / IP** — freedom-to-operate risk surfaced during patent research (see `patent-applications.md` if one exists), or risk of a competitor patenting around the idea.

For each risk:

- **Description**: concrete, specific — not "the market might not want this."
- **Likelihood**: low / medium / high, with the reasoning in one line.
- **Impact**: what happens to the business if it materializes.
- **Mitigation**: what reduces the likelihood or impact.
- **Monitoring / trigger**: the concrete signal that means this risk is materializing (e.g. "CAC exceeds $80 for two consecutive months," "a competitor announces a product in this exact niche") — so it's watched for, not just written down once.

## Sources

- Every source used to identify or size a risk, so the assessment can be re-verified or deepened later.
