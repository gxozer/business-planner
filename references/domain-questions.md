# Domain-Specific Intake Questions

Round-two intake (see `SKILL.md`) shouldn't run the same generic question list for a SaaS product and a physical-goods business — what matters for pricing, operations, and risk is genuinely different by business-model domain. Use this to pick the right set instead of one-size-fits-all.

Identify the domain from the idea description itself; ask directly only if it's genuinely ambiguous (e.g. "is this closer to a software product, a physical product, or a service?"). Many ideas span more than one domain — a hardware product with a companion app, a service business built on a software platform — pull questions from each relevant domain rather than forcing a single bucket.

## SaaS / Software Product

- Pricing model: per-seat, usage-based, or flat subscription?
- Self-serve signup, or sales-led onboarding?
- What churn/retention benchmarks do comparable products see?
- Integration or API requirements — what does it need to plug into?
- Security/compliance needs — SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2), HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) — given the target customer?
- Anything requiring specialized engineering, or is it buildable on existing infrastructure?

## Physical Product / E-commerce / CPG

- Manufacturing: in-house, contract manufacturer, or white-label?
- Unit economics: cost of goods sold, target margin, minimum order quantities?
- Distribution: direct-to-consumer, retail, wholesale, or a mix?
- Who handles fulfillment and shipping, and at what cost?
- Regulatory requirements for the product category — FDA (Food and Drug Administration), CPSC (Consumer Product Safety Commission), food safety, etc.?
- Packaging and returns handling?

## Marketplace / Platform

- Which side — supply or demand — is harder to acquire first, and how does that chicken-and-egg problem get solved?
- Take rate or commission model?
- Trust and safety: verification, dispute resolution, payment handling?
- What creates liquidity — how many participants before it feels "alive" to a new user?

## Service Business

- Billing model: hourly, retainer, project-based, or productized?
- Delivery capacity: how many clients per person, and what's the plan to scale past founder time?
- Client acquisition: referral-based, outbound, or content/inbound?
- What differentiates this from a freelancer or agency already doing it?

## Hardware / Physical + Software

- Current stage: concept sketch, working prototype, or pre-production?
- Bill-of-materials cost at target volume, and how it changes with scale?
- Certification requirements — UL (Underwriters Laboratories), CE (Conformité Européenne), FCC (Federal Communications Commission), etc. — for the target market?
- Manufacturing/tooling lead time and minimum viable order size?
- Supply chain dependencies and single points of failure?

## Content / Media / Creator

- Primary distribution channel, and how the audience gets acquired?
- Monetization mix: ads, subscriptions, sponsorships, affiliate, product sales?
- Content production cadence and cost per piece?
- What's defensible here that a competitor can't just copy?

## Fallback — idea doesn't fit cleanly

Use the base round-two topics from `SKILL.md` (business model, pricing, go-to-market, operations, team, financials), and treat anything domain-specific that turns up during research as a prompt for an adaptive follow-up instead of a missed question.
